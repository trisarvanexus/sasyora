Our Story

Rooted In Indian Agriculture

Sasyora™ was born from a simple belief — nature provides the purest nourishment.

We work with responsible farmers and trusted agricultural communities to bring authentic, naturally refreshing products directly to consumers.

Our first launch, Elaneer, reflects our commitment to purity, quality, freshness and sustainability.

Coconut Capital

Why Pollachi?

Located near the Western Ghats in Tamil Nadu, Pollachi is widely known for its coconut farms.

The combination of fertile soil, tropical weather, abundant sunshine and generations of farming expertise makes Pollachi ideal for growing tender coconuts.
