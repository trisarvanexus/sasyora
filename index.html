Inspired by Pollachi

Pollachi, nestled near the foothills of the Western Ghats in Tamil Nadu, is widely known for its lush coconut groves and favorable tropical climate. Its fertile soil, warm weather, and abundant rainfall make the region synonymous with high-quality coconuts.

Sasyora™ draws inspiration from this heritage of coconut cultivation and the timeless freshness associated with India's coconut-growing regions.
